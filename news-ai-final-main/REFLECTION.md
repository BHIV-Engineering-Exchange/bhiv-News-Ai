# Reflection

## Humility
- Recognize limits and iterate transparently
- Seek feedback and adapt pipeline thresholds
- Favor evidence via metrics over assumptions

## Gratitude
- Appreciate collaborators and upstream services
- Acknowledge user guidance and clear scope boundaries
- Value constructive critique that improves reliability

## Integrity
- Keep APIs consistent and truthful in responses
- Avoid overpromising; document fallbacks and retries
- Protect keys and data; log only essentials
